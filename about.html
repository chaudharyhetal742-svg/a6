Atelier Provenance

Generational Mastery in Haute Weaving & Slow Fashion

Preserving the endangered art of the handloom while engineering tactile masterpieces for the world's most exacting fashion houses.

The Genesis of TextileCharm

Founded on Broome Street in the heart of New York's historic Cast Iron textile district, TextileCharm was conceived as an uncompromising sanctuary for true fabric craftsmanship. While modern fashion accelerated into polyester blends and disposable garments, our founders set out to rescue vintage American, British, and Japanese shuttle looms, restoring them to mechanical perfection.

We believe that a garment's elegance is determined long before the first scissor cuts through pattern paper. It is embedded in the tension of the warp yarns, the density of the hand-thrown shuttle, and the natural elasticity of unadulterated botanical and animal fibers.

Our Fiber Sourcing Manifesto

We work exclusively with ethical shepherds and organic farmers who practice regenerative pasture rotation. Our ultra-fine merino wool originates from generational farms in New South Wales where sheep graze on diverse native grasses. Our cashmere is hand-combed during springtime molting from free-roaming nomadic herds in the harsh Alashan plateau of Inner Mongolia.

Furthermore, our European flax linen is dew-retted across the foggy coastal fields of Normandy, where the natural moisture of the Atlantic allows soil microbes to gently loosen the fibers without harsh chemical detergents, producing a lustrous, durable bast fiber celebrated for millennia.

Our Unshakable Oath: Zero synthetic fibers, zero plastic microfibers, and zero heavy metal dye mordants. Every single yard of TextileCharm fabric is 100% biodegradable and safe for both the artisan and the Earth.

The Living Atelier Today

Today, our Broome Street flagship functions as both a design studio and an active archive housing over 3,000 historical weave bindings, antique swatch folios, and botanical dye test formulas. Here, couture designers, master Savile Row tailors, and independent sartorialists collaborate directly with our master weavers to develop custom cloths that define landmark runway collections and bespoke heirlooms.
