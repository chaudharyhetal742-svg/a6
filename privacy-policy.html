Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified by TextileCharm Legal Compliance Directorate

1. Commitment to Client Privacy and Data Stewardship

TextileCharm operates under an unwavering corporate commitment to safeguarding the personal dignity, confidential communications, and proprietary designs of every fashion house, bespoke client, and retail patron who interacts with our digital atelier. We recognize that custom tailoring inquiries and luxury textile specifications frequently involve sensitive commercial blueprints and personal anatomical measurements that require stringent institutional data protection protocols. When you navigate our digital portal, request artisanal swatch folios, or commission custom woven textiles, we collect only the necessary technical and logistical data required to execute our textile services with flawless precision. We strictly reject unauthorized third-party commercial profiling and never monetize, trade, or distribute your identity to outside marketing conglomerates.

2. Categories of Information Collected and Processing Rationales

To deliver our bespoke swatch books and facilitate custom textile runs, TextileCharm collects personal identification data including your full name, professional fashion studio affiliation, postal delivery address, electronic mail address, and direct telephone contact details. In instances where bespoke tailoring consultations or custom weaving commissions are initiated, our master artisans may additionally document specialized cloth preferences, garment silhouette measurements, and intended collection release schedules to ensure precise production scheduling. Technical usage metrics such as Internet Protocol addresses, browser agent signatures, and session interaction timestamps are captured automatically via encrypted protocols exclusively to maintain portal stability and optimize user experience across modern desktop and mobile platforms.

3. Secure Payment Architecture and Financial Privacy

All financial settlements, bespoke commission deposits, and swatch dispatch fees conducted through TextileCharm are processed exclusively through certified Level-1 PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways equipped with modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. TextileCharm does not store, process, or maintain unencrypted credit card numbers, card verification values, or confidential banking credentials upon our private local servers or internal atelier databases. Financial interactions are routed directly through vetted, bank-grade merchant processing partners who adhere strictly to international monetary regulations and financial fraud prevention mandates, ensuring that your commercial transactions remain entirely impenetrable against unauthorized interceptors or cyber threats.

4. International Data Transfers and Regulatory Compliance

Given that our fine textiles and seasonal swatch folios are dispatched to haute couture design houses spanning Paris, Milan, London, Tokyo, and New York, your personal information may occasionally be transmitted across national borders to fulfill courier logistical chains and legal customs declarations. When cross-border transmissions occur, TextileCharm enforces rigorous Standard Contractual Clauses and aligns all internal data handling routines with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). This unified regulatory framework guarantees that your fundamental privacy rights remain robustly protected regardless of the specific geographical jurisdiction where our fabric bolts are delivered or where our encrypted servers reside.

5. Your Statutory Rights and Atelier Privacy Contact

As a valued client and patron of TextileCharm, you maintain the absolute statutory prerogative to inspect, modify, download, or permanently expunge any personal details stored within our active atelier directories at any time upon formal written request. Should you wish to review your historical fabric order records, revoke prior consent for seasonal lookbook dispatches, or enforce your legal right to digital erasure, our dedicated Data Protection Officer is prepared to fulfill your request within thirty business days. You may formally submit any privacy inquiries, data modification notices, or regulatory requests directly via electronic mail to our compliance division at compliance@textilecharm.com or via registered mail to our SoHo flagship located at 482 Broome Street, New York, NY 10013.
