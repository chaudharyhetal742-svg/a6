Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified by TextileCharm Legal Compliance Directorate

1. Fundamental Principles of Digital Cookie Utilization

TextileCharm utilizes small alphanumeric text files known as cookies, along with related browser storage mechanisms, to ensure the reliable operational functionality, responsive interface navigation, and secure interaction of our digital textile portal. When you visit our website, these micro-files are transferred to your local computing device or mobile terminal to enable our system to recognize your individual browser session, preserve your active fabric swatch selections, and retain your accessibility preferences between distinct page visits. Our implementation of cookie technology is grounded in strict data minimization principles, ensuring that we deploy only those identifiers that are genuinely essential for presenting our haute textile collections with optimal visual clarity and cryptographic security across modern internet environments.

2. Strict Separation of Essential and Performance Cookies

Cookies deployed upon the TextileCharm portal are classified into two distinct technical categories: strictly necessary functional cookies and privacy-preserving aggregate performance cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are technically vital for enabling basic portal features, such as maintaining active navigation states across our textile archives, operating our interactive fabric drape calculator, and processing digital swatch reservation forms securely through our encrypted server stack. Performance cookies collect completely anonymized, aggregated telemetry regarding visitor traffic flow, popular journal treatises, and mobile screen resolution distributions without capturing personally identifiable user data, enabling our technical engineers to systematically enhance loading speeds and eradicate responsive layout anomalies.

3. Absolute Rejection of Invasive Third-Party Tracking Pixels

Unlike conventional commercial e-commerce websites that deploy invasive third-party tracking pixels, cross-device behavioral surveillance cookies, and intrusive retargeting advertising scripts, TextileCharm strictly prohibits exploitative surveillance software from operating within our digital atelier domain. We firmly believe that the exploration of artisanal fashion and heritage weaving should remain an uncompromised, contemplative experience entirely free from aggressive commercial tracking or unwanted digital marketing solicitations. We do not sell, rent, or distribute any cookie telemetry generated during your visit to external advertising networks, data brokers, or commercial syndicates under any circumstances whatsoever.

4. Autonomous Browser Management and Cookie Deactivation

Every visitor to TextileCharm maintains comprehensive, autonomous control over the storage, retention, and elimination of digital cookies via the integrated preference menus of their respective web browsing applications. You possess the continuous technical option to configure your browser to reject all incoming cookie requests, alert you before an identifier is written to your local disk, or automatically expunge all cached cookies upon concluding your active browsing session. Should you elect to disable strictly necessary cookies, you may continue to read our long-form fashion journal guides freely; however, certain dynamic features, including the interactive drape calculator and digital swatch request forms, may experience degraded operational capabilities.

5. Policy Updates and Regulatory Communication Channels

TextileCharm periodically reviews and updates this Cookie Policy to reflect ongoing technical enhancements to our digital infrastructure and ensure continuous alignment with evolving global privacy regulations, including the European Union ePrivacy Directive and regional data security mandates. Any substantive modifications to our cookie deployment protocols or privacy practices will be transparently documented upon this page alongside an updated legal effective date at the top of the instrument. If you have specific technical questions regarding our cookie utilization or wish to obtain further clarification regarding our data minimization standards, please contact our web governance team directly via electronic mail at compliance@textilecharm.com.
