2. Nature of Artisanal Textiles and Material Variations

Every bolt of cloth created by TextileCharm is crafted utilizing traditional low-speed shuttle looms, organic unbleached yarns, and artisanal botanical dye formulations that celebrate the natural character of authentic plant and animal fibers. Consequently, patrons and bespoke tailoring clients acknowledge that minor natural irregularities—including subtle slub textural variations, gentle selvedge undulations, and organic hue nuances between disparate botanical dye lots—do not represent manufacturing defects, but rather constitute the authentic hallmarks of genuine handloom weaving. While our atelier team strives for absolute consistency against approved client strike-offs, minor dimensional shifts of up to three percent may naturally manifest across varying humidity conditions due to the inherent breathability of natural wool, linen, and silk fibers.
